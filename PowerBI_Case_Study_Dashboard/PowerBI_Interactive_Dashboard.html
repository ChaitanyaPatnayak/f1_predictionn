Qualifying & Pole-to-Win Determinism (Q1 – Q3)

Evaluating how strongly Saturday grid rank dictates Sunday victories across circuits and eras.

Q1: Starting Grid vs Finishing Position Reject H0 (p < 0.001)
Two-Sample Welch t-Test (t = 5.186, p = 3.09e-07): Grid determinism rose from r = 0.662 in early F1 to r = 0.749 today.
Q2: Hybrid Era Pole Advantage A/B Test Fail to Reject (p = 0.400)
Two-Proportion Z-Test (Z = 0.841, p = 0.400): Hybrid era (53.6%) vs V10/V8 era (49.8%) difference is +3.8% and statistically negligible.
Q3: Circuit Pole Conversion Rates (with 95% CIs) Fail to Reject (p = 0.621)
Chi-Square Test (chi2 = 0.245, p = 0.621): Monaco pole conversion (46.5%) is statistically indistinguishable from other tracks (42.7%). True highest track is Barcelona (~70%).

Team Dynasties & Car vs. Driver Variance (Q4 – Q5)

Quantifying monopoly power in Formula 1 and decomposing the relative importance of car vs driver.

Q4: Team Dominance Trend (HHI 1980–2025) Reject H0 (p = 0.0146)
OLS Slope = +46.8 points/year (r = 0.358, p = 0.0146): F1 win concentration is genuinely rising. Effective winning teams collapsed from 5–6 in the 1980s down to only 1–2 today.
Q5: Car vs Driver Variance Decomposition Reject H0 (p < 0.001)
Two-Way Fixed Effects ANOVA: Car explains ~60.0% of race outcome variation, while driver talent accounts for only ~4.5%.

Mechanical Reliability & Pit Stop Strategy (Q6 – Q7)

Analyzing how engineering reliability eliminated mechanical failures and transformed pit execution.

Q6: DNF Causes Over Decades Reject H0 (p < 0.001)
Multi-Class Chi-Square Test: Mechanical retirements collapsed from 40% in the 1970s down to under 10% today.
Q7: Pit Stop Consistency & Dispersion (IQR) Reject H0 (p = 0.0012)
IQR Trend Correlation: Refueling ban in 2010 drove pit stops from 8+ seconds down to sub-2.5 seconds with tight standard deviation.

Driver Career Peak, Home Advantage & Talent (Q8 – Q10)

Examining age/experience curves, national driver pipelines, and testing the home race myth.

Q8: Driver Career Peak (Age vs Experience) Reject H0 (p < 0.001)
Teammate Logit Model: Quadratic age curve proves driver peak occurs at ~28–32 years with 6–10 years experience.
Q9: Home Grand Prix Advantage Fail to Reject (p = 0.420)
Paired t-Test (t = 0.81, p = 0.420): Drivers perform no better at their home race than at neutral tracks against teammates.
Q10: Driver Talent by Nationality Pipeline Reject H0 (p = 0.002)
Exact Binomial Tests + Benjamini-Hochberg FDR: Identifies significant historical driver clusters in UK, Germany, Brazil, and Finland.

Regulations & Track Architecture (Q11 – Q13)

Evaluating the 2022 Ground-Effect overhaul, street vs permanent tracks, and title clinch timing.

Q11: 2022 Ground-Effect Regulation A/B Test Reject H0 (p = 0.0035)
Two-Sample Welch t-Test: Net position changes per race increased by +2.4 positions after 2022 aerodynamic reform.
Q12: Street vs Permanent Road Circuits Reject H0 (p < 0.001)
Two-Sample Welch t-Test: Street tracks exhibit 34% lower overtaking and 2.1x higher safety car probability.
Q13: Championship Clinch Timing Curves Reject H0 (p = 0.001)
Cumulative Points Gap Threshold Model: High dominance eras seal titles with 3–5 rounds remaining.

AI Predictive Modeling & Strategy Insights (Q14 – Q15)

Forecasting driver finishing positions and calculating marginal counterfactual strategy gains.

Q14: Predictive ML Model Evaluation R² = 0.60 | MAE = ±2.5

Q15: Pre-Race Feature Importance & Strategy Grid Beta = 0.46
